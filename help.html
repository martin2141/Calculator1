Four-Function Calculator Guide

A four-function calculator handles basic math: addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division.

Basic Functions

  • C / CE (Clear / Clear Entry): Clears the current number or resets the calculator.
  • + (Addition): Adds two or more numbers together.
  • - (Subtraction): Subtracts the second number from the first.
  • × (Multiplication): Multiplies numbers together.
  • ÷ (Division): Divides the first number by the second.
  • = (Equals): Shows the final answer.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Turn on the calculator or press C to clear the screen.
  2. Type your first number using the number keys.
  3. Press your operation button (like + or ×).
  4. Type your second number.
  5. Press = to see your result.
